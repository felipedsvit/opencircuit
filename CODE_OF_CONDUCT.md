# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in OpenCircuit to be a respectful experience for everyone,
regardless of experience level, background, identity or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific in technical discussions.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions before escalating.
- Accept review feedback and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Respect the project's safety limits: OpenCircuit is not a certified design or
  validation tool.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior in issues, reviews or discussions.
- Encouraging unsafe use of the software as a substitute for professional
  engineering.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation for behavior that violates this code. Reports should be
made privately to the maintainers; do not open a public issue containing personal
or sensitive details.

This code is inspired by the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
